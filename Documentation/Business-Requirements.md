# Business Requirements Document

## Retail Sales Performance & Executive Decision Support System

**Version:** 1.0  
**Role:** Data Analyst  
**Tool:** Microsoft Excel

---

## 1. Project Overview

The objective of this project is to develop an interactive Executive Decision Support System in Microsoft Excel that transforms retail sales data into meaningful business insights.

The solution uses Power Query, advanced Excel formulas, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, slicers, timelines and VBA automation to support sales, profitability, customer, product and regional analysis.

---

## 2. Business Background

The business requires a centralized reporting solution to monitor sales performance, profitability, customer behaviour and regional performance.

Management needs an interactive reporting solution that can provide timely information for business decision-making.

---

## 3. Business Problem

Traditional reporting can be manual and time-consuming. Without a centralized interactive dashboard, executives may find it difficult to monitor important business KPIs and identify trends across sales, customers, products and regions.

---

## 4. Project Objectives

The project aims to:

- Automate recurring reporting activities
- Build an executive-level interactive dashboard
- Monitor important business KPIs
- Analyze sales performance and trends
- Analyze customer performance
- Analyze product performance
- Analyze regional and market performance
- Support data-driven business decisions

---

## 6. Key Business Questions

The dashboard is designed to answer questions such as:

- What are the overall sales and profit levels?
- Which region performs best?
- Which products are most profitable?
- Which customer segment contributes the most revenue?
- How do discounts affect profitability?
- How are sales changing over time?
- Which categories and markets contribute most to sales?
